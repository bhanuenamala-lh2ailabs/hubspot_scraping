# OutFlo Leads — Ready to Call

**Where:** HubSpot → Deals → **Campaign** pipeline. All **282** leads are assigned to you, tagged **"Outflo Outreach – India"** or **"– Indonesia."** These are people we messaged on LinkedIn via OutFlo.

## Call in this order — use the `Priority` field
- 🔴 **High (12)** — they **replied** to our LinkedIn message. Hottest, call first. **Their reply is in the deal Note — read it before dialing.**
- 🟠 **Medium (53)** — they **accepted our connection request** but haven't replied. Warm, call next.
- 🟡 **Low (64)** — connection request **sent 2+ days ago, not yet accepted**. Cooler, call after High/Medium.
- **(No priority)** — already carry a status from the tracker sheet, or a booked meeting.

## Stage tells you the state
- **Cold Call** → these are the ones to dial.
- **GMeet Fixed** → meeting already booked (prep, don't cold-call).
- **Interested / Script Shared / Call Attempted** → already in progress.
- **Dead** → skip.

## On each lead you'll find
- **Phone number** (where we found one — ~70% have one) + **LinkedIn URL**
- A **Note** with: their **reply text**, when they **connected / replied**, and which account (Ashish/Yash) messaged them
- **India / Indonesia** tag

## Workflow
1. Sort the Campaign board by **Priority (High → Low)**.
2. Open the lead, **read the Note** (reply text + history).
3. Dial the phone number.
4. **Log the call** and **move the stage** (Interested / GMeet Fixed / Dead-with-reason).

*Leads are refreshed from OutFlo periodically; new repliers/connectors will appear at the top by priority.*
