Deal Flow
1. Entry Point
Begin Here → Cold LinkedIn outreach is sent to the prospect.
2. Profile PreScreen LinkedIn 
→ Wrong Fit / Not Contacted: Dead/Cold/WrongFit — screened from profile, not contacted.
→ Good Fit : Move to Cold LinkedIn — Initial Response step.
3. Cold LinkedIn — Initial Response
The prospect's response to the cold LinkedIn message determines the next step:
→ No Reply: Move to Message Back step (cold email + second LinkedIn message).
→ Reply: Move to Replied step
3.1 Message Back (Cold Email + Second Message)
Sent 1–2 days after no reply to the first LinkedIn message.
→ No Reply: Dead/Cold/No Reply.
→ Reply Received: Return to Cold LinkedIn step for re-evaluation.
4. Replied
Once the prospect responds as interested:
→ Agreed but Ghosts: Move to Replied step
→ Not Interested: Dead/Cold/Not Interested.
→ Books Meeting: Move to Gmeet Fixed step.
4.1 Message Back (Ghosts)
Sent 1–2 days after no reply to the first LinkedIn message.
→ Not Interested: Dead/Cold/No Reply.
→ Reply Received: Return to Replied step for re-evaluation.
5. Gmeet Fixed
Paste the Gmeet link and hand ownership to the designated owner. Proceed to the After the Gmeet decision point once the meeting has taken place.
6. After the Gmeet
→ Wrong Fit: Dead/Interested/No Show.
→ Privacy Concerns: Dead/Interested/No Show.
→ Proceeds: Move to One Pager + Company Deck Shared by Us.
7. One Pager + Company Deck Shared by Us
We share the one pager and company deck with the prospect, then move to internal evaluation.
8. One Pager Received + Internal Evaluation for Sample Collection
→ Wrong Fit: Dead/Sample Not Collected/Wrong Fit-Rejected.
→ Good Fit: Move to Request Company for Samples.
9. Request Company for Samples
→ Sample Not Received: Move to Follow Up step.
→ Sample Received: Move to Company Sends Sample + Interest Gauge step.
9.1 Follow Up (Email + Call Back), 1–2 Days
→ Sample Not Received: Dead/Sample Not Received/Company No Show.
→ Sample Received: Move to Company Sends Sample + Interest Gauge step.
10. Company Sends Sample + Interest Gauge with Demand — Handover to Owner
→ Wrong Fit: Dead/No Interest from Demand/Wrong Fit-Rejected.
→ Good Fit: Move to Token Amount Paid Step.
11. Token Amount Paid 
 A token amount is paid once the sample is received to build trust and put skin in the game. 
12. Commercial Negotiations
→ Price Gap: Dead/Negotiations/Pricing.
→ Contract Terms: Dead/Negotiations/Contractual.
→ Terms Agreed: Move to Deal Contract Sign.
13. Post-Agreement Steps to Close
12.1. Deal Contract Sign
12.2. Data Migration Done
12.3. Payment Initiation — enter Deal Cost
12.4. Closed/Won — client email + internal email sent
Dead-End Outcomes Reference
Quick reference for every terminal (non-progressing) outcome in the flow:
Outcome
Trigger
Dead/Cold/WrongFit
Screened from profile, prospect never contacted.
Dead/Cold/Not Interested
Prospect responds not interested to cold LinkedIn message.
Dead/Cold/No Reply
No reply after cold email + second LinkedIn message.
Dead/Interested/No Show
Prospect agrees but ghosts before the meeting, or is a wrong fit / raises privacy concerns after the Gmeet.
Dead/Sample Not Collected/Wrong Fit-Rejected
Internal evaluation of the one pager finds the prospect a wrong fit.
Dead/Sample Not Received/Company No Show
Sample still not received after the follow-up step.
Dead/No Interest from Demand/Wrong Fit-Rejected
Sample and interest gauge come back as wrong fit.
Dead/Negotiations/Pricing
Commercial negotiations stall on a price gap.
Dead/Negotiations/Contractual
Commercial negotiations stall on contract terms.
